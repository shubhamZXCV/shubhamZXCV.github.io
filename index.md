## About Me

Hi! I'm Shubham, a final-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Hyderabad.

I love building **scalable, distributed systems**: software that keeps working as data, traffic and the number of machines grow, and as some of those machines fail. I've explored this through projects like a Kafka-inspired replicated event log and a distributed network file system. Closer to the hardware, I spent summer 2026 as a System Software Engineering Intern at NVIDIA, working on GPU drivers.

I'm also into **machine learning and computer vision**. As an undergraduate researcher at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/), advised by Prof. C. V. Jawahar, I work on detecting AI-generated images and tracing which model produced them. I also co-authored *How Does India Cook Biryani?*, an oral presentation at ICVGIP 2025.

Away from the keyboard, you'll find me at the gym, out on a run or on the football field. I'm also learning the guitar and catching up on anime.
